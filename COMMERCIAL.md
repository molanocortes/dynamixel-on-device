## When you need a commercial license

If AGPL-3.0's copyleft does not fit your use, for example because you want to embed this code
in a proprietary or closed-source product and are not willing to release your own source
under AGPL, then a separate **commercial license** is available. It lifts the AGPL copyleft
obligations for your product.
